Pocket Invoice – Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · App: Pocket Invoice (com.wlktech.invoiceoffline) · Developer: WLK-TECH

Englishမြန်မာ

Summary

Pocket Invoice works offline. Your business profile, logo, customers, items, quotations, invoices and receipts are stored only on your phone. We have no server and no account system, and we never receive or see this data.

Data you enter

  • Everything you type into the app stays in the app's private storage on your device.
  • PDFs are created on the phone. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself (for example through Viber, Telegram or email).
  • Backup files are saved only where you choose (Downloads, Google Drive and so on). We have no copy.
  • Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup if device backup is turned on in your phone settings. That backup is managed by Android and Google, not by us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following with Google:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAdvertising, measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Advertising, fraud prevention
Ad interactions and app diagnosticsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent encrypted. It does not include anything you typed into your documents. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Buying Pocket Invoice Pro removes all ads.

Purchases

Pocket Invoice Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your Google account.

Updates

The app uses Google Play to check for new versions. This check is handled by Google Play.

Children

Pocket Invoice is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete documents, customers or items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: wlktech22@gmail.com
